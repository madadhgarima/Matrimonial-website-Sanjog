Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sanjog verify profiles?
Every profile on Sanjog undergoes a multi-step verification process including Government ID check (Aadhaar/Passport/Driving License), contact number OTP verification, and educational degree validation. Profiles that complete all verifications receive our green Verified Badge.
Is my photograph and phone number private?
Absolutely. Sanjog provides complete photo and contact privacy controls. You can choose to blur your photo to unregistered visitors, reveal your telephone number only to accepted interests, or keep your details private upon request.
Can parents create and manage a profile?
Yes! Over 45% of Sanjog profiles are managed by loving parents and siblings. During signup, you can simply select "Creating profile for Daughter/Son" and provide candidate details alongside parent contact numbers.
What is Compatibility Matching on Sanjog?
Our platform integrates personality, lifestyle, education, family values, and preferences to calculate compatibility for lifelong harmony and mutual respect.
